Light is the sport

Bathroom mirrors and dark cave lighting kill matches

The mirror is not candid. The yellow bathroom bulb is not “moody.” Cave lighting is how you disappear on a six-inch screen.

Why the mirror dies

Phone in the glass. Tiles. Toilet in the corner. Flash on the mirror. She is not dating your apartment’s grout. She is deciding if your face is worth a second photo.

A bathroom selfie says you had ten seconds and no window. That is not mysterious. That is the last photo you should ever put first.

The dark cave

Bedroom with one lamp. Bar with no face. Sunset behind you so you are a silhouette. Low light plus phone noise equals mush. Mush does not get matches. It gets a polite skip.

What to shoot tonight

  • Face the window. Not the ceiling light. Overhead makes raccoon eyes. Daylight on the face is the whole trick.
  • Kill the flash in glass. If you can see the phone, she can see the phone.
  • Eyes in the thumbnail. Pinch the photo to stamp size. If the eyes are gone, delete it.
  • One lamp is not a vibe. It is a cave. Turn on another light or go outside.
